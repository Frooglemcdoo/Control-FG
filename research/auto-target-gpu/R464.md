R463 four-ray and ten-ray runs both retain red speckles. Neither is a clean
visual reference. Four-ray 161232 timing means: .565001/.948456/.050575 ms
(ray/evaluator/guide), 139 samples. Ten-ray 161932: 1.375999/2.171471/.057972 ms,
24 samples. Not frame matched. No halts or invalid audit pixels.

Four-ray top 96 logged examples all outside both guide spheres at weight 2.
Ten-ray 59/60 outside at ~1.666667; one covered/downweighted example, eight
miss materials. Surface outlier distances to nearest guide: four-ray min .253,
median .429, max .620 m; ten-ray max .408 m. Several largest points share a
patch around (-82.2,25,107.1). Material IDs are secondary-hit/run-local IDs;
brightness outlier summaries do not identify visible red pixels by themselves.

Refinement of the previous guide-selection hypothesis: input guides DO select
this patch, often near (-82,25.008,106.91), but dense fit reaches the .25 m floor.
Accumulate learns footprint only within the chosen hash-cell key. Neighboring
parts of the same physical patch can be omitted at cell boundaries.

R464 Freeze retains top-two ranking and original observed anchors. For a dense
chosen cell (count >=8), lanes scan other occupied cells and consider anchors
within cellSize*1.5 (currently .75 m) whose raw peak energy is >=25% of the chosen
peak. Bound radius includes their anchor distance plus observed bright extent
and existing .05 m padding, capped at cellSize*1.6 (.8 m). Existing single-cell
fit is a lower bound. Sparse, compact, dim-only and distant-source cases retain
previous behavior. No cell-index neighborhood cutoff: physical distance avoids
another grid-seam artifact. Hash collisions can still discard support.

Only Freeze shader changes. No sampling/ray count/weight/lighting changes.
Work: two additional bounded scans over 4096 cells plus group reduction. Need
live guide timing to measure overhead. Wider bounds may increase variance for
already covered sources, so this is a candidate with explicit tradeoffs.

Tests: actual GPU builder covers representative captured cross-cell geometry,
rejects dim and distant support, preserves observed anchors and all existing
compact/sparse/duplicate/collision fixtures. Synthetic neighboring-light tests
compare narrow vs expanded bounds at both ray counts for mean energy and tail
variance. These tests are not a replay of missing full live training records.
