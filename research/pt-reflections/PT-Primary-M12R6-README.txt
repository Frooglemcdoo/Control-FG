Control-FG primary PT M12R6 - binding admission repair

Close Control. Replace the included files beside Control_DX12.exe, keeping
Streamline and the rest of the existing installation in place.

F10 opens the overlay. Use PT Reflections OFF/ON to compare the same view.
Leave F11 in normal mode. Note FPS and whether the lighting changes.
After closing Control, run Collect-PT-Primary-M12R6.cmd and upload its ZIP.

R5 recovered FPS but submitted no primary PT dispatch. This build fixes
silently discarded binding updates from previously unregistered command-list
interfaces. COM aliases share the existing canonical recording state. The
normal setter path keeps one lock and performs no repeated QueryInterface.
R5 history compaction and OFF-mode performance changes are retained.

The collector includes early native-route and compute-pass counters, so a
remaining blocked pass is distinguishable from submitted rays with zero hits.
The existing reflection and glass shaders are unchanged.

Windows compilation, WARP and in-game results must be reported separately:
passing a fixture does not establish that PT is active in Control. Actual
submission and positive GPU coverage are required from the in-game run.
