Control FG v2.1.2 adds DLSS Frame Generation, Multi Frame Generation, Dynamic MFG and Ray Reconstruction to supported Steam, Epic Games Store and GOG DX12 builds. This update fixes major RR VRAM retention during live enable/disable, E/F and resolution changes; adds Experimental Texture Streaming controls (Off / 4 ms / 6 ms / 8 ms) plus an optional VRAM monitor; and adds RTX 40-series NVIDIA 617.14 / DLSS-G 310.9.1 compatibility for experimental 3x-6x MFG. Higher texture budgets can cause traversal stutter. The RTX 40 compatibility patch loads only when experimental MFG is enabled before startup and stays active until restart. Special thanks to speedlemur for the core RR integration approach and HotKnives for hardware QA.
