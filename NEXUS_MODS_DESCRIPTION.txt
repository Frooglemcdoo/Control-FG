Control FG v2.1.1 adds DLSS Frame Generation, Multi Frame Generation, Dynamic MFG and Ray Reconstruction to supported Steam, Epic Games Store and GOG DX12 builds. This update adds experimental Texture Streaming controls (Off / 4 ms / 6 ms / 8 ms), an optional VRAM monitor, and a major RR VRAM-lifetime fix. Higher texture budgets can cause traversal stutter. RTX 40-series MFG remains experimental; current NVIDIA 617.14 / DLSS-G 310.9.1 reports can reject 3x+ while 2x remains the fallback. Special thanks to speedlemur for the core RR integration approach and HotKnives for hardware QA.
