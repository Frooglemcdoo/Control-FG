Control The Oldest House - R386

INSTALL
Close Control. Copy this entire package beside Control_DX12.exe, preserving folders.

CHECK SETTINGS
F10 > Capture > CHECK SETTINGS.
The panel closes. After five seconds, one beep starts a ten-second check.
Keep Jesse visible and play or pan slowly. Do not change settings or switch apps.
Two beeps mean the ZIP has finished packaging; Explorer selects it. Send that ZIP.
Keep the game open until the two beeps. No administrator prompt or external recorder.

The small ZIP contains SUMMARY.txt, starting/ending runtime settings snapshots,
saved mod settings and renderer.ini when available, and saved-file copy status.
It records RR evaluation results, Owned/native GI dispatches, character/volume/
lighting bindings, clamp invocations, RELAX recording/submission/GPU completion,
and existing FG runtime state. Missing scene coverage is marked NOT_OBSERVED.
Errors and fallbacks are reported even if other frames succeeded. Settings changes
or loss of focus mark the observation incomplete. No universal ALL PASS is issued.

Saved options are not proof of active execution. Native quality, DLSS/HDR, sample
counts, glass, storage transfers and VRAM monitoring are configuration-only unless
SUMMARY explicitly supplies runtime evidence. A status snapshot may be stale; only
the collection-window event counts establish observed activity in that window.
This cannot certify image quality or replace a performance comparison.

No screenshots, textures, ETW/WPR traces or shadow inventory are collected.
Extra counters are enabled only during the check; no automatic capture at startup.
Partial files are retained at LOCALAPPDATA/ControlFGProbe/Settings-R386-*.
Packaging failure/timeouts retain the folder and report an error.

RENDERING AND SETTINGS
R384/R385 rendering behavior is preserved. RR enables Owned GI, NRD RELAX, four
indirect samples, Native reflection sample policy and clamp60 automatically.
Visual settings retain Enhanced Character Lighting under Quality, Enhanced Lighting
and Shadows under Ray Tracing, and FPS Counter under Display/Monitor.
Defaults: character On, lighting On, FPS Off. These selections persist.
F10 retains on-demand captures and advanced comparisons, including GI RAW and
RESET COMPARE. Capture tools remain idle until explicitly requested.

Validation: CPU tests check gating, stale-generation rejection and verdicts.
The Windows workflow compiles the DLL and runs the existing rendering/menu tests.
In-game visual quality, feature coverage and performance require gameplay testing.
