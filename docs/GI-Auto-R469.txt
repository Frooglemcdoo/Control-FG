R469 - GI dispatch ownership and guide-builder cost

Install beside Control_DX12.exe. Both launchers use the same DLL:
ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Start-GI-Auto-R469-4Rays.cmd
ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Start-GI-Auto-R469-10Rays.cmd
GI FIXED, RR ON, RELAX OFF, FG OFF for comparable performance measurements.
Restart with the other launcher to change the ray count.

R468's 4-ray log measured guide construction at 1.36 ms mean / 2.66 ms peak
(23 samples outside capture). R469 divides each receiver's candidate list into
32 parallel lists and reduces their winners. Exact candidate priority and
primary/secondary selection remain unchanged. No extra rays or temporal blur.
Normal launchers now clear the forced DRED diagnostic flag used for the freeze
investigation. This is not a measured live FPS improvement yet.

Accepted replacement ray dispatches bypass native rays; accepted replacement
GI6 evaluations bypass native GI6. The legacy owned post-dispatch replay is now
explicitly excluded after any replacement. Required geometry, hit/miss material
work, and downstream reconstruction remain. Bounded 'bypass' log counters record
native dispatch suppression at the hooks. Rejected admission retains fallback.
The R468 log did not establish that duplicate native GI caused the FPS loss.

No F7 for the performance run: stage timings and bypass counts log automatically.
Play the same area and collect compact logs. Residual speckles are not claimed
fixed by this cost/ownership change; their input-direction flags remain under
investigation. Capture-box movement remains Alt+arrows, not Ctrl.
