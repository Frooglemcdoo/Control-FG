R444 - Skip zero-contribution point-light shadow lookups

Uses R442's evaluator and combined buffer reads. R443 constant-read hoisting
is removed. The point-light shadow block now checks the same saturated
surface/light cosine used by the final native lighting calculation. When
that cosine is zero, it skips the shadow lookup. The light list, range,
attenuation, BRDF, accumulation, spotlights and all ten GI rays remain.
No history reuse, blur or light-distance cutoff is introduced.

A Windows GPU fixture compares native and gated point-light calculations
across 65,536 finite cases with varied normals, positions, light parameters,
shadow flags and procedural shadow moments. Accumulated RGB matches bit for
bit. This fixture is not a full-scene rendering or hardware performance test.
Check Jesse, plants, lamps and moving camera lighting for unchanged quality.

Close Control and extract beside Control_DX12.exe, replacing files.
Use RR ON, FG OFF, GI RAYS 10, GI GUIDE ON (FIXED), RELAX OFF, CONTACT AUTO.
Keep REF CLAMP at 60%.

F10 -> GI COST CAPTURE compares R442 and R444 at ten rays throughout.
Stay still for about two minutes; the ZIP opens in Explorer on completion.
The first four phases form an R442 / R444 / R444 / R442 bracket. The primary
summary comparison uses only that bracket; later phases check stability
and query overhead. Actual evaluator binding is recorded in every phase.
The capture restores ten rays and the R444 evaluator on completion/abort.
No in-game performance gain is claimed until measured on the target GPU.
