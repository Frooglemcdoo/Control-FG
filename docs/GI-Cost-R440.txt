R440A - GI ray-cost capture on the accepted R434/R439 rendering baseline

This is a measurement build, not a performance optimization.
Rendering shaders, sampling and filtering are unchanged from R439.

Close Control and extract beside Control_DX12.exe, replacing files.
Use RR ON, FG OFF, RELAX: OFF, GI: FIXED, GI RAYS 10 and your accepted lighting settings.
Stand at the lamp/Jesse view, press F10, and click GI COST CAPTURE.
One immediate beep confirms the capture was accepted. If a message appears,
the capture did not start; it names the blocking requirement.
Return to gameplay and keep the camera still for about two minutes.
The capture automatically repeats Native and 10 rays while retaining other
settings. It also measures 10 rays without GPU timing queries.
Two beeps and an Explorer window indicate the ZIP is ready. Send that ZIP.
The original ray selection (10) is restored on completion or an aborted run.

Reports include effective ray counts, rendered frame cadence, GI tracing plus
hit-data writes, GI evaluation plus hit-data reads, and RR GPU timings.
This does not independently isolate memory traffic within those stages.
Capture is on demand; no profiling runs during ordinary gameplay.
Do not use the temporary Native phases to judge 10-ray quality.

R440A fixes R440 rejecting RELAX: OFF (manual GI mode 4). Rendering is unchanged.
