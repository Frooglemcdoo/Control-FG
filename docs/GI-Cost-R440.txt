R441 - Skip unused one-ray guide work at multi-ray counts

This candidate keeps all 10 GI samples, ray directions, ranges, hit/miss
shading and RR settings. The fixed one-ray guide remains unchanged.
At counts above one, the ray shader skips 213 guide instructions whose
results were previously calculated then discarded. The GPU driver may
already optimize some of this work; performance gain is not yet measured.
No blur, clamp, temporal reuse or sample reduction is added.

Close Control and extract beside Control_DX12.exe, replacing files.
Use RR ON, FG OFF, GI RAYS 10, GI GUIDE ON (FIXED), RELAX OFF, CONTACT AUTO.
Check the accepted lamp/Jesse view for the same clean image.
F10 -> GI COST CAPTURE measures the same Native/10-ray phases as R440A.
An immediate beep confirms acceptance. Return to gameplay and remain
still for about two minutes. Two beeps and Explorer identify the report ZIP.
Send that ZIP. Ten rays are restored on completion or an aborted run.

R440A's capture-start fix and specific failure messages are retained.
