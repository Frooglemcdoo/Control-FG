R461 - Retired scratch reuse, 10-ray and 4-ray candidates

Install beside Control_DX12.exe. Close the game before switching packages.
Run the included ControlTOH\Diagnostics\Start-GI-Auto-R461-NRays.cmd.
The launcher selects the actual GI ray count for that session through the native
producer getter, before allocation and shader binding. It does not edit your
saved settings. RR and GI FIXED must be enabled. Keep RELAX and FG OFF for the
comparison. The saved menu sample selection may differ from the launch override.

10 rays: retain R460 proposal allocation (2+2 guided, 6 general rays).
4 rays: experimental allocation (1+1 guided, 2 general rays), matching PDFs and
four-ray averaging. Same learned guide builder; no additional blur or clamp.
Four-ray visual quality is unverified and may be noisier than the ten-ray build.

Both versions reuse private training/cell buffers and upload ray records only
after both GPU completion and command-list recording retirement. Published guide
buffers remain immutable and separately allocated. Scratch reuse saves three
resource creations per normal frame after warmup; capture frames use fresh
storage. No GPU waits are added. FPS improvement is not yet measured in-game.

For FPS comparison, play without pressing F7. Compare the same scene, resolution,
camera and settings against R460. The orange box is optional for log capture:
Alt+arrows moves it; F7 arms a five-second countdown. Keep the neck/face inside
it until Capture complete, then run Collect-ControlFG-Compact-Logs.cmd.
No Ctrl movement input is used. Capture summaries can affect frame pacing.
