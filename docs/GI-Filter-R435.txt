merging ControlTOH. Launch normally, not with a previous diagnostic launcher.
Keep RR, RT indirect lighting and Enhanced Lighting enabled.

and the native configured spatial passes before RR. This is the historical
clean-but-ghosting reference, not a new ghosting fix or an exact R100 binary.

test BOTH first at the lamp/plant/hair location, standing still and moving.
Report speckles, motion trails, GI spotlight shadows and performance.

pre-R434 GI ray/evaluator selection and spotlight shaders. No saved sample
count, reflection clamp, lighting, or RR setting is overwritten.
No RELAX filter is added on top of BOTH. Native spatial pass count is preserved.
Compact logs retain filter-stage application records for verification.
