ten rays use two guided and eight cosine rays. At four rays the guide sample
chooses either guide equally, rather than permanently neglecting one guide.
The producer and evaluator share the same mixture PDF and frozen selection.
Maximum uncovered-direction weights become 4/3 and 5/4 instead of 2 and 5/3.
This is a sampling-density change, not a brightness clamp or history filter.
It trades some sampling of accurately guided sources for broader coverage.

No extra rays or GPU passes. Replaced native dispatches remain bypassed;
R469 parallel guide construction and disabled forced DRED are retained.
This does not solve guide selection completely or prove whole-game quality
or FPS improvement. Test the known speckle area and a second area.
