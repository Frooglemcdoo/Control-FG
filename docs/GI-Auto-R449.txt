R449 - Stratified automatic GI guidance: experimental test build

R448 was reported to reduce FPS without fixing speckles. Do not retest R448.
This candidate changes the estimator, not performance tuning. Its image quality
and performance require in-game validation; compilation is not a visual pass.

Extract beside Control_DX12.exe with Control closed. Run
ControlTOH\Diagnostics\Start-GI-Auto-R449.cmd.
Use TEN GI rays, GI FIXED, RR ON, RELAX OFF, FG OFF for comparison.
A normal launch leaves automatic guidance disabled for a same-build baseline.

With valid frozen targets, all ten fresh rays use balance weights. The mixture
selector is stratified: two rays per target cone and six cosine rays. No pilot
ray receives the old random residual coefficient. Full RGB including misses is
weighted; no radiance clamp, blur, temporal radiance reuse or extra ray is added.
Empty/invalid targets preserve the existing ten-ray sampling.

This can reduce rare-hit variance when targets cover the responsible source.
It cannot guarantee a useful guide: the current learner still selects two global
cells. Stale or irrelevant targets can increase noise, and constant fields are
preserved in expectation rather than exactly per pixel. No FPS gain is claimed.

Confirm GI_AUTO_R449 producer and evaluator lines in compact logs; the producer
identifies estimator=stratified_balance. No such lines means this candidate was
not demonstrated active. A halted=1 line means pairing failed; do not interpret
that session as a valid visual comparison. Restart normally for the baseline.
A partial pairing failure can skip the current GI pass and halt new admissions.

Use the existing compact-log collector after comparison. No new large source
capture is requested. Existing capture-box movement remains Alt+arrows.
