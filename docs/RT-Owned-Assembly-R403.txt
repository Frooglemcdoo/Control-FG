CONTROL TOH R403 - OWNED AS COMMAND RECORDING

Close Control, extract into the game directory and replace the previous test files.
Keep your usual graphics settings. Play normally, including movement and combat;
check moving objects, destruction, reflections and lighting. After closing Control,
run ControlTOH\Tools\Collect-RT-Assembly.cmd and send RT-Assembly-R403.zip.

R403 replaces the native AS command-recording job body with owned orchestration.
It preserves queue/thread initialization, dynamic-provider callbacks, buffer
transitions, BLAS callbacks, parity-selected postbuild copies, current material-ID
patching, the TLAS call, and deferred cleanup publication in their native order.
The native body is suppressed at its verified job tail. Each job still records
exactly one TLAS build. There is no duplicate scene or second AS build.

Native GPU build routines, resource allocation/retirement and preparation joins
remain in use. This is another ownership stage, not a full AS backend replacement
or a promised FPS gain. PTLAS and persistent prepared-record caching stay disabled.

Sparse CPU timing separates thread setup, dynamic updates, BLAS/finalization,
material-ID patching, and TLAS/finish work. Timings are cumulative over timed_jobs
(one job in sixteen), not GPU durations or per-frame costs. The existing logs
measure the outer recording job but do not separate these internal costs.

Expected: RT_ASSEMBLY_R403_INSTALL, RT_SELECTION_R403_INSTALL,
RT_PREPARE_R403_INSTALL and RT_RECORD_R403_INSTALL ready=1.
RT_CACHE_R403_INSTALL ready=0 disabled=1 remains intentional.
