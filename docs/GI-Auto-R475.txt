R475 - Secondary shading terms (diagnostic build)

Combined four/ten-ray package. Start with:
ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Start-GI-Auto-R475-4Rays.cmd
GI FIXED, RR ON, RELAX OFF, FG OFF.
Alt+arrows moves the capture box; Alt+Insert starts the five-second countdown.
Keep Jesse's affected face/hair inside the box, reproduce the speckles until
Capture complete, and send compact logs. Never press F7.
Ten-ray launcher is included; one four-ray run first is enough.

Sampling, guides and native shading instructions remain R473/R474.
This is a diagnostic, not a proposed visual fix. It adds no rays/passes.
For the same ray selected by R474's largest weighted red contribution,
red_term records material texture/tint, volume, surface volume after BRDF,
point and spot light contributions, and the native orange volume branch.
There is no separately added emission term in this native evaluator block;
emissive content may be represented in upstream/baked lighting. These records
do not establish scene-material names or identify a defective term by color.
The volume sentinel (volume info x = 0xffffffff) selects RGB (1,.35,0).
Its intended meaning is not established; no fallback color has been changed.

Term RGB / aux definitions:
texture: material RGB texture / branch (1 miss, 2 shaded surface, 3 negligible material)
tint: material coefficient / number of participating sentinel-volume branches
volume: normalized volume lighting plus ambient / total volume blend weight
surface: volume after distance fade, BRDF and 1/pi / distance fade
point: point-light sum before material / number of visited point entries
spot: total direct minus point sum / number of visited spot entries
sentinel: weighted orange contribution before normalization / sentinel weight sum
brdf: volume BRDF multiplier / miss multiplier (branch 1 only)
Surface secondary ~= texture*tint*(surface+point+spot); miss secondary = volume*brdf.aux.
Spot subtraction and reconstruction may have float-rounding differences.

Red candidates can be legitimate red scene content. RR inputs/output are not
captured; this is not an end-to-end RR trace or proof that RR is uninvolved.
Capture records now use 192 bytes/pixel (64 original + 128 term bytes), with
matching readback: 96MiB per 512x512 ROI for this tail alone. No extra capture
allocation/readback outside capture. Diagnostic shader register overhead is
unmeasured. Use non-capture timing separately; no FPS improvement is claimed.
