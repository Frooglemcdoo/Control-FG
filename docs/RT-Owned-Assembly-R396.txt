CONTROL TOH R396 - OWNED MATERIAL APPENDS

Close Control and extract over your existing installation (R395).
Keep your current graphics settings and native ray count. Launch normally.
Play your usual test route, moving between rooms and destroying some objects.
Check reflections, shadows, glass, Jesse and streaming as you normally would.
Close Control and run ControlTOH\Tools\Collect-RT-Assembly.cmd.
Send RT-Assembly-R396.zip and report any visual change, crash or performance change.
No capture hotkey or new standalone GPU test is needed.

This replaces the native per-object record assembly routine at its three checked
callsites. It assembles the instance and geometry records and material references
once. Native geometry providers, object selection, material/resource updates,
allocator growth and downstream acceleration-structure builds remain in use.
The native object assembly routine is not called after successful installation.
Verified-capacity append paths now cover both 64-byte records and 8-byte material
pointers. Per-object telemetry no longer reads a shared atomic counter. Full
buffers still use the native growth allocator. No extra GPU passes are added.

This is a partial scene-preparation takeover, NOT the complete CPU-preparation
replacement. PTLAS remains disabled in-game. There is no promised FPS gain.
The owned routine is tested against the exact original native machine-code body
for 4096 cases, including NaNs, masks, material overrides, geometry bytes, prefix
counters, provider call order and growth. In-game visual validation is still needed.

The log reports RT_ASSEMBLY_R396_INSTALL ready=1 and active=1 when running.
An unsupported renderer or conflicting callsite is left on the native path.
The R393 automatic instance capture is disabled in this build.
