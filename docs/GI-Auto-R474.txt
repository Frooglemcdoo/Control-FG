R474 - Red contribution source trace (diagnostic build)

Install beside Control_DX12.exe preserving folders. One DLL, two launchers:
ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Start-GI-Auto-R474-4Rays.cmd
ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Start-GI-Auto-R474-10Rays.cmd
Keep GI FIXED, RR ON, RELAX OFF, FG OFF. Start with four rays.

Rendering equations, sampling allocation and source guide construction remain
R473. This build is for evidence, not another attempted visual fix.
Alt+arrows moves the box; Alt+Insert starts the 5-second countdown.
Keep the affected face/hair area inside the box and reproduce the speckles
until Capture complete. Send the compact logs. Never press F7.

The capture independently ranks locally isolated red pixels, rather than
only the brightest luminance peaks. For each selected pixel it records the
ray contributing the most weighted red excess, actual producer direction,
hit material ID/position, secondary-shading RGB, receiver throughput RGB,
raw ray RGB, sampling weight, fade, raw mean RGB and final GI RGB.
A red candidate may be real red scene content; this does not classify errors.
The selected red ray may differ from the separately logged luminance peak.

Scope: source/evaluator GI only. These records are not the bound RR inputs
or reconstructed output and must not be represented as an end-to-end RR
trace. They determine whether red outliers originate in this GI stage; a
matched RR-boundary capture remains necessary to attribute reconstruction.

Capture adds a 64-byte record per ROI pixel and matching readback (~32MiB
extra at 512x512 per in-flight capture). No such allocation/readback outside
capture; no extra rays/passes. Shader register cost remains to be measured.
Normal rendering is not intentionally changed; no FPS gain is claimed.
