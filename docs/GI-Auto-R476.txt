R476 - Full-screen GI/RR scene diagnostic. No proposed visual fix.

Install the contents beside Control_DX12.exe. Start-GI-Auto-R476-4Rays.cmd
and Start-GI-Auto-R476-10Rays.cmd share one DLL. Begin with four rays.
GI FIXED, RR ON, FG OFF, RELAX OFF. Close other capture workflows.
Alt+Insert starts the sequence; never press F7. No box placement is needed.
Hold still for the first consecutive pair. When the label reports the next
pair is queued, slowly pan through the lighting that produces the speckles.
Wait for Saved, then run ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Package-GI-Scene.cmd.
Send scene-manifest.zip and every scene-part ZIP. Compact logs alone omit images.
A new sequence requires restarting the game after the two pairs are saved.

Captured at full resolution, not resized/cropped: hit material/normal/UV/position
for each active physical ray slice; raw owned GI immediately after evaluation;
temporal-filter source before composition; GI at RR input; accumulated diffuse
and specular lighting; bound RR color/depth/motion/normal/albedo/specular/distance
where available; reconstruction map; hair mask where bound; RR output. Exposure,
jitter, projection and frame metadata accompany each frame.
Detailed R475 terms and actual producer directions/weights use a uniform grid
of up to 512x512 points across the entire screen, matched to those frames.
Local-red candidate ratios on this grid describe grid neighbours, not adjacent
full-resolution pixels. Full images remain available for dense pixel analysis.

Two consecutive pairs are captured. There is a settling/export gap between
pairs; they are not a continuous four-frame video. Timing outside capture is
logged separately. Large readbacks and disk writes can cause temporary stalls;
do not use capture FPS as a performance measurement. Full-resolution capture
can consume several GB; the packager streams bounded ZIP parts without deleting
source captures. Never bypass a game fence or force resource-state guesses.

Limits explicitly recorded: physical hit slices are captured but SRV view
mapping is not independently proved; per-light operands, full texture mip
contents, and instance/primitive IDs are not exported. Hit UVs and sampled RGB
are available, but this alone does not prove texture binding correctness.
RR output is captured before final display processing, not a desktop screenshot.
A capture must pass frame/output/completeness checks before being called complete.
