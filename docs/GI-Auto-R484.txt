R484 motion diagnostic - not a speckle fix

Extract beside Control_DX12.exe, replacing existing files. Run ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Start-GI-Motion-R484.cmd. Uses 4 rays and pre-R483 sampling. Normal 4/10-ray launchers are included. R483 receiver-facing rejection is removed.

GI FIXED, RR ON, FG OFF, RELAX OFF. Move Jesse through the problematic lighting. Press Alt+Insert once while moving. Keep moving until STOP appears, then release movement and camera input. Wait for Saved, close the game, and run Package-GI-Motion.cmd. Never F7. No movement key is injected.

Records 16 consecutive evaluated frames with full-screen RR inputs/output, raw GI, hit slices, exact frozen guides, actual ray origin/direction/weight, each ray's RGB contribution, secondary RGB, throughput/fade, and eight full-precision lighting/material terms. The system constants are saved per frame. Every pixel and ray is recorded; no threshold determines which samples survive. RR private internal history is not accessible.

The 16-frame window is bounded, not a multi-second video guarantee. The STOP cue occurs after frame 4; metadata records frame times. The review tells us whether the event and movement-to-rest transition were actually captured. File completeness alone is not proof that a speckle was captured. Capturing perturbs frame timing substantially; never use it to judge FPS.

Large raw data: approximately 60 GiB at 2560x1440 internal / 4 rays, plus packaging space. Preflight conservatively checks RAM/disk and 32-bit shader addressing. Higher resolutions/ray counts may not fit; capture fails explicitly instead of cropping/skipping. If preflight fails, package diagnostics and send its log, not a new game run.

All files remain until you delete them. The packager validates every frame, all ray stamps/weights and GI reconstruction, creates a review, and hashes ZIP chunks. It reports numerical anomalies separately from missing/corrupt data. It does not claim to inspect RR's internal history or individual shadow-map taps.
