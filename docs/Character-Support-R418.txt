ControlTOH R418 - Character activation logging repair

This is an observability repair, not a claimed noise fix. Rendering is unchanged
from R417. RELAX remains off by default.

The submitted R417 log contained only 13 RT lifecycle lines. The clean-build
diagnostics gate suppressed the skin and hair replacement activation messages,
so their absence could not establish whether either replacement activated.

R418 records one startup marker and the existing bounded skin/hair replacement
creation events during ordinary play. It does not enable continuous diagnostics,
performance recording, GPU readbacks, or capture passes. Each replacement list
is capped at 32 entries; cached records do not log again.

Install over the existing mod while the game is closed. After ordinary play in
the affected scene, send the newest probe log from %LOCALAPPDATA%\ControlFGProbe.
No capture button, comparison sequence, or video is required for this check.

An activation event proves a replacement record was created, not that its pixels
reached the final RR output or that it resolves the observed noise.
