R478 - Producer-weight correction and secondary-albedo filtering comparison.
One DLL supports 4 and 10 rays. Native GI ray/evaluator bypass remains in place.

Close the game; extract beside Control_DX12.exe, keeping folders intact.
ControlTOH/Diagnostics/Start-GI-Auto-R478-4Rays.cmd is the corrected mip-0 baseline.
Start-GI-Auto-R478-4Rays-Mip1.cmd selects a fixed mip-1 secondary-albedo comparison.
Equivalent 10Rays launchers are included. Restart between modes. The ordinary
launchers explicitly clear the mip-1 environment switch.
GI FIXED, RR ON, FG OFF, RELAX OFF. Keep camera/settings comparable between runs.

The evaluator now reads the original producer's FP32 weight for every ray. It
no longer independently regenerates production weights. A UAV barrier separates
producer writes from evaluator reads. Buffers follow existing submission/fence
retirement and scratch reuse; no game fences are bypassed.

Mip1 only changes the secondary hit's base-color texture lookup. It does not
alter primary material detail, projection lights, shadows, BRDF lookup, or the
native reference translation used elsewhere. This is a diagnostic comparison,
not a validated ray-cone/footprint filter and not a claim of a speckle fix.
A texture without coarser mip levels may show no difference. Mip0 is the default.
Both modes include the weight correction so their visual difference isolates
secondary albedo mip selection.

Measure FPS outside capture. The new full-resolution weight allocation costs
16 bytes/pixel at four rays or 40 bytes/pixel at ten rays for each in-flight
invocation (about 56/141 MiB at 2560x1440 or 127/316 MiB at 3840x2160).
The evaluator avoids repeated production sampling calculations; net frame-time
impact is unmeasured in the game. At most two retired scratch allocations are
cached; the invocation retention guard is 4 GiB, not a reservation.

Full-screen capture remains Alt+Insert: hold still for pair 1, slowly pan for
pair 2, wait for Saved. Never press F7. Run Package-GI-Scene.cmd and send the
manifest plus every part ZIP. Each session records the mip mode in metadata.
Captures can stall briefly and consume several GB. Restart for another sequence.
Full-resolution GI/RR images and active hit slices are saved; detailed terms
use a 512x512 grid. Per-light operands, full texture mip contents and instance/
primitive IDs are not exported. Use compact logs if capture fails.
