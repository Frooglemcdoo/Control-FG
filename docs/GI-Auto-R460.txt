R460 - Movable capture box

Install beside Control_DX12.exe and run
ControlTOH\Diagnostics\Start-GI-Auto-R460.cmd.
Use 10 rays, GI FIXED, RR ON, RELAX OFF, FG OFF. Use windowed/borderless mode
so the Windows capture outline can appear over the game.

An orange square marks the exact 512x512 GI-input region (scaled to your game
window and DLSS input resolution). Hold ALT and press ARROW KEYS to position it.
No Ctrl input is used. The outline is click-through and never takes focus.
Press F7 once when the box covers the remaining speckles. A five-second countdown
starts. The box locks for the full capture sequence. Stand still for 20 seconds,
then pan slowly until the status says Capture complete. Keep the issue inside
the box. Collection no longer expires while you are positioning the box.

Ten snapshots/five adjacent pairs are saved to compact logs. After completion,
you may move the box and press F7 again without restarting. Run
ControlTOH\Tools\Collect-ControlFG-Compact-Logs.cmd and send the ZIP.
The box hides when you switch to another application or minimize the game.
No huge files or desktop screenshots are written. The box is not drawn into GI
or RR buffers. R460 keeps observed-hit centers and the shared sampling seed.
Dense training cells fit their guide radius to hits at least one quarter as
bright as the anchor, with 5cm padding and a 25cm minimum radius. The radius
never exceeds the previous 43.3cm. Cells with fewer than eight training hits
retain the original wide radius. These distances scale with the cell size.
This concentrates the existing ten rays on compact bright patches. R459 target
selection and all radiance weights remain unchanged. No blur, clamp, extra
rays or lighting history. Live improvement still needs testing.

Each invocation freezes the selected ROI coordinates for its GPU shader and
log records. Bounds clamp to the input image. Temporal pairing rejects different
origins as well as different dimensions/versions. Repeated captures reset their
pair cache. Extra diagnostics can affect pacing; this is not an FPS benchmark.
