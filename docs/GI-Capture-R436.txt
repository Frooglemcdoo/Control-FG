R436 - BOTH filter image capture (R435 rendering unchanged)

Close Control and extract beside Control_DX12.exe, replacing files.
Launch normally. RR, RT indirect lighting and Enhanced Lighting ON; FG OFF.
Use 4 GI rays and GI: BOTH. Keep these settings throughout the capture.
Frame the weak projector light and a surface that boils while moving.
Open F10, click CAPTURE BOTH and return to gameplay before the five-second delay.
One beep: hold still. Three beeps: pan slowly. Two beeps: the ZIP is ready.
Explorer selects the completed ZIP. Send it.

Records two consecutive full-resolution frames stationary and two moving,
with pre-filter GI, filtered GI at RR input, HDR RR input, RR output, guides,
motion data and filter-stage validation. No GPU timings are claimed. The filtered
image is allowed to differ from the raw image. RAW capture checks remain strict.
There is no separate image immediately after the first temporal pass.
Readbacks can pause the game; these frames are not steady-state FPS measurements.
No guided sampling, ray-count change, new filter or quality fix is enabled.
