R470 - Actual producer ray/weight capture (diagnostic build)

Install beside Control_DX12.exe; both 4/10 launchers are in ControlTOH/Diagnostics.
Use GI FIXED, RR ON, RELAX OFF, FG OFF. Forced DRED remains disabled.
This build does not claim a noise or FPS fix.

Normal play retains R469 sampling and guide construction. The existing capture
box now also records the actual producer origin, direction and weight for every
ray inside the box on sampled capture frames. The evaluator compares those
values with its reconstruction and checks the returned hit against the actual
producer direction. No producer-packet allocation or readback outside capture.

Place the box over the speckles with Alt+arrows, then press Alt+Insert to start
the 5-second countdown and capture. Collect compact logs afterward. Do not use F7. Capture
frames have extra GPU storage/readback cost; compare FPS outside the capture.
New producer_contract lines separate frame/record identity, weight disagreement,
direction disagreement and actual-ray-to-hit disagreement. These measurements
are needed before attributing the R469 flags to sampling, transforms or hit data.
