ControlTOH R421 - Full-resolution motion capture

R420 did not fix the jaw/neck noise or red hair speckles. This build does not
claim another visual fix. It adds a focused capture to locate the unstable
signal before further rendering changes.

CAPTURE MOTION replaces the old GI-path comparison button. It requires the
existing Native Blend / RR ON / FG OFF / RELAX OFF configuration and no active
lighting diagnostic override. It does not change lighting or switch conditions.
Keep the camera/character moving under the GI illumination that exposes the
problem. One beep arms the capture; two beeps mean the ZIP is ready.

The capture records one consecutive pair at full resolution: raw GI before its
filter boundary, GI at RR, accumulated diffuse/specular lighting, HDR scene
input, depth, motion, material/normal buffers, RR guides and RR output. The
large per-ray hit dump is excluded. There is no stationary comparison phase.

Capturing requires full-sized GPU readbacks and disk output, so it can affect
frame timing. It is not a performance benchmark or zero-overhead recording.
No capture runs continuously. It does not include the native packed character
reconstruction map or effective per-fragment coverage; those remain separate
evidence gaps. Both frames must have matching boundaries and consecutive IDs.
Incomplete pairs are reported as failures rather than successful packages.

Install with the game closed. Existing rendering remains R420, whose coverage
guard was visually ineffective in the user's test. Preserve projector/desk/
plant appearance when investigating the remaining GI-lit character artifacts.
